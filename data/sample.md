# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (Sample)

## Compensation

The salary cap for individual contributors is $100,000 per annum, reviewed every
October. Bonuses are capped at 15% of base salary and are paid in the first pay
cycle after the review window closes.

## Leave Policy

Employees accrue 1.75 days of paid leave per completed month of service. Unused
leave carries over to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days; any
balance beyond that lapses on 31 December.

## Remote Work

Remote work is permitted up to three days per week with manager approval.
Employees working from a different country for more than 30 consecutive days
must notify the People Operations team in advance for tax compliance reasons.

## Expense Reimbursement

Expenses below $50 do not require a receipt. Anything above that threshold must
be submitted with an itemised receipt within 30 days of the transaction date.
Reimbursements are processed on the 15th of each month.
